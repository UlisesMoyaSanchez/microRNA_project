# Citation review — manuscript/jbi/main.tex

**Purpose.** Every citation in the JBI manuscript, cross-checked against its primary source:
what does `main.tex` actually assert near the `\cite{}`, and what does the source itself say?
`references.bib` already carries a verification `note` on most entries from an earlier pass
(2026-08-11/12), but those notes mostly confirm **bibliographic metadata** (author list, venue,
DOI) via web search/Crossref/DBLP — not that the source's content supports the specific claim
made in the prose. This table closes that gap.

**Scope: all 40 unique citation keys used in `main.tex`** (`grep`'d across the whole file,
handling multi-line `\cite{}` blocks — a first pass with a line-only regex missed 23 keys
embedded in a multi-line citation list; corrected here). 41 rows: 22 are the literature-survey
papers (Table 3 in the manuscript), whose link + verified quote already exist in
`results/literature_survey.tsv` from primary-source verification done this session and a prior
one — reformatted here, not re-fetched. The other 19 were freshly fetched and read this pass
(arXiv/DOI/proceedings pages, Semantic Scholar API, PMC, OGB's own leaderboard).

**Assessment key:** ✅ Supported — the source states what the sentence claims. ⚠️ Caveat —
supported but with a nuance, a narrower/broader scope than implied, or a detail I could not
independently re-confirm this pass. ❌ Mismatch — not found in the review.

**Net finding: no mismatches.** Two rows carry a caveat worth the authors' attention before
submission (`briere2026kgeleakage`'s specific permutation-test detail, and
`almusawi2023review`'s fit as evidence for a specific numeric range); everything else held up
against the actual source text.

## What this review does not cover

This is a citation-content check, not a re-audit of the manuscript's own experimental numbers
(those are already covered by `results/EVALUATION_AUDIT.md` and `results/HMDD_TOPOLOGY_AUDIT.md`)
or of the survey's single-rater limitation (tracked separately as the manuscript's fifth
Discussion limitation). Nothing in `main.tex` or `references.bib` was modified in this pass —
this is a read-only artifact for manual review; any fixes it suggests (the two ⚠️ rows above)
are a follow-up decision for the authors.
